The process

How ModelMoving helps organize your moving quote request.

Long-distance moves can get confusing fast. ModelMoving gives customers a route-focused place to submit the details movers usually need before reviewing options.

Choose the closest route page

Start from the page that best matches your pickup area, destination, or specialty route.

Submit your move details

Add pickup and delivery ZIPs, move date, home size, contact info, access notes, packing needs, and special items.

Your request is organized around fit

Route coverage, pickup and delivery markets, shipment size, timing, access, packing, storage, specialty needs, and moving-partner availability can all affect which options may fit.

Selected moving partners may follow up

If a moving partner appears to fit the route and requested services, that partner may review the request and contact you about its own estimate, availability, service details, and pricing.

Trust & methodology

What ModelMoving does for moving customers.

Clear expectations matter. ModelMoving is designed to help customers organize route-based moving quote requests and connect with selected moving partners whose services may fit the route and move details.

ModelMoving organizes the request

Pickup and delivery locations, move timing, household size, access, packing, storage, specialty items, and other details can be submitted together so the move is easier to review.

ModelMoving may help match by fit

Potential fit can depend on route coverage, requested timing, shipment size, service needs, origin and destination access, and available moving partners.

The moving partner provides the estimate

ModelMoving does not guarantee a price or booking. The moving partner reviewing the move is responsible for its estimate, services, availability, agreements, payment terms, and final pricing.

How ModelMoving evaluates moving options

ModelMoving does not claim that every moving company is suitable for every move. When considering potential route fit, factors can include origin and destination coverage, move timing, household size, packing or storage needs, specialty items, access requirements, and current moving-partner availability. Customers should independently review the provider and written terms before booking.

What affects quote options?

Long-distance moving quotes depend on more than mileage.

Mileage matters, but it is only one part of a long-distance move. Shipment size, route demand, pickup access, delivery access, packing, storage, dates, and availability can all affect quote options.

Pickup and delivery access

Apartment walk-ups, elevators, parking limits, gated communities, long carries, and building restrictions can change the moving plan.

Service needs

Packing, storage, fragile items, large items, disassembly, or special handling may affect availability and pricing.

Dates and flexibility

Month-end dates, summer moves, short notice requests, and tight delivery windows can affect which options may be available.

Planning range methodology

How ModelMoving creates planning ranges.

Selected route pages include price ranges to help customers understand the possible scale of a move before speaking with a moving provider.

Planning guidance, not a quoted price

ModelMoving planning ranges are informed by nationwide moving-sales experience and common route-pricing patterns. They are not guaranteed prices, binding estimates, or published market averages. Actual estimates can change based on inventory, mileage, route, pickup and delivery access, packing, storage, move dates, specialty services, and moving-partner availability.

When a moving partner provides an estimate, that provider's written estimate and service terms control — not a planning range shown on ModelMoving.

FAQ

Questions about how ModelMoving works.

These answers explain the basics of the ModelMoving quote request process.

What is ModelMoving?

ModelMoving is a moving quote request and matching platform. It helps organize customer move details and may connect customers with selected moving partners based on route, timing, availability, and move details.

Is submitting a request free?

Yes. Submitting a moving quote request through ModelMoving is free and there is no obligation to purchase services.

What information should I include?

Helpful details include pickup city or ZIP, delivery city or ZIP, move date or window, home size, inventory, packing needs, storage needs, stairs, elevators, parking access, and special items.

Will I receive a guaranteed price immediately?

No. A request helps organize your information. Final pricing depends on move details, service availability, route, shipment size, access, timing, and provider pricing.

How does ModelMoving decide which moving options may fit?

Route, pickup and delivery markets, requested timing, shipment size, access, packing, storage, specialty requirements, and moving-partner availability can all affect potential fit.

How are ModelMoving planning ranges created?

Planning ranges shown on selected route pages are informed by nationwide moving-sales experience and common route-pricing patterns. They are planning guidance only, not guaranteed prices or published market averages.

What should I review before booking a mover?

Review the provider's legal company identity, applicable licensing or registration, written estimate, insurance or valuation options, pickup and delivery terms, payment terms, cancellation policy, and any storage or specialty-service terms.

Can I use ModelMoving for specialty routes?

Yes. ModelMoving includes support pages and route pages for state-to-state moves and specialty routes such as Hawaii, Alaska, and Puerto Rico.
