Moving cost guide

How much does a Northeast long-distance move cost?

Long-distance moving costs vary based on exact pickup and delivery ZIP codes, inventory size, access conditions, packing needs, storage, move date, and route availability. The ranges below are broad planning ranges informed by nationwide moving-sales experience, not guaranteed prices or published market averages.

Studio / 1 Bedroom

$1,800–$4,500+

Smaller moves may still vary based on distance, timing, stairs, elevators, packing, and minimum shipment requirements.

2 Bedroom

$3,000–$6,500+

Pricing depends heavily on furniture volume, route, building access, parking, and whether packing or storage is needed.

3 Bedroom

$4,500–$8,500+

Larger residential moves require more inventory detail, clearer access notes, and more route planning.

4+ Bedroom / Large Move

$7,000–$12,000+

Full household moves may involve more labor, packing, storage, specialty items, and longer planning windows.

✓
These ModelMoving planning ranges are intended to help customers understand the possible scale of a Northeast long-distance move. Moving partners reviewing the actual route and inventory provide their own estimates, services, availability, agreements, and final pricing.
