Route details

Florida to New York moves can involve very different access conditions at each end.

Florida gated communities, condos, and suburban pickups can differ sharply from New York high-rises, walk-ups, parking restrictions, and building time windows.

Florida pickup details

  • Miami, Fort Lauderdale, West Palm Beach, Orlando, Tampa, and Jacksonville pickups
  • Gated-community and HOA access rules
  • Condo elevator reservations
  • Storage-unit and seasonal-move pickups
  • Naples, Sarasota, Fort Myers, and nearby Florida markets

New York delivery details

  • New York City parking and loading restrictions
  • Walk-ups, elevators, and building move windows
  • Brooklyn, Queens, the Bronx, and Staten Island deliveries
  • Long Island and Westchester destinations
  • Storage or delayed final delivery when needed
